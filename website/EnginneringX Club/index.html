Our mission

Engineering is a way of learning by doing.

EngineeringX helps students develop practical engineering skills through experimentation, project development, teamwork, and learning from mistakes. We create space for curiosity to become real systems, prototypes, and ideas that are worth testing.

Engineering disciplines

Where ideas cross disciplines.

These are the club’s areas of interest — not formal departments or claims of institutional structure.

Robotics & Mechatronics

Systems, motion, control, sensing, and physical intelligence.

Electronics & Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers, sensors, control logic, and reliable hardware interfaces.

Mechanical Engineering

Materials, mechanics, structures, motion systems, and real-world constraints.

Software & Computing

Embedded code, simulation, data, optimization, and connected systems.

CAD & Prototyping

Modeling, fabrication workflows, iteration, and physical testing.

Electrical Engineering

Power, circuits, energy flow, and dependable systems design.

Architecture & Design

Design thinking, spatial systems, form, and how engineering decisions shape user experience.

Engineering process

From question to working system.

This is our general approach to project work — a flexible process used to guide learning and iteration, not a claim that every project has completed every stage.

01 — research

Research

Clarify problems, map constraints, and define what matters before building.

02 — design

Design

Sketch directions, model systems, and test concept viability with purpose.

03 — prototype

Prototype

Turn the chosen idea into a tangible build that exposes real-world tradeoffs.

04 — test

Test

Observe performance, gather evidence, and learn from failure or unexpected behavior.

05 — improve

Improve

Refine the design, improve reliability, and move the project toward a stronger solution.
